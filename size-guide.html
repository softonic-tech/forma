Size guide

Measurements are body measurements in inches. If you sit between sizes, size up — scrubs should move, not bind.

Scrub size chart in inches
Size Chest Waist Hip Top length Inseam
XS 32–34 24–26 34–36 25 29
S 34–36 26–28 36–38 26 29
M 38–40 30–32 40–42 27 30
L 42–44 34–36 44–46 28 30
XL 46–48 38–40 48–50 29 31
XXL 50–52 42–44 52–54 30 31
  • Chest Measure around the fullest part of the chest, tape level under the arms.
  • Waist Measure at the natural waist, where the pant drawstring will sit.
  • Hip Measure around the fullest part of the hip and seat.
  • Unsure? Send your chest and waist on WhatsApp and we will recommend a size before you order.
