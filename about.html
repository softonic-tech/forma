About

Uniforms made for the people who wear them every day.

Scrubs Ilyas makes and sells medical-college uniforms — stretch-woven sets that survive a full rotation, not just a photo.

Who we make for

Medical college students spend more hours in uniform than in anything else they own. We cut scrubs for that life: lectures, skills lab, ward rounds, and the wash that follows.

The line is small on purpose — unisex and women’s sets, a matching top, and a cargo pant — in teal, navy, and olive. Those are the colors colleges actually ask for. Custom college colorways and class sets are available on request.

How they are made

Each piece is sewn in a soft-matte stretch-woven cloth chosen to move with the body and hold dye through repeated washing. Pockets sit where students put a pen, a phone, and an ID. The pant has an elastic waist and a drawstring so the fit stays put through a twelve-hour shift.

How you buy

There is no checkout cart yet. You pick a size and color on the site, then send the order on WhatsApp. We confirm stock, price, and delivery with you before any payment. That keeps the first version of this shop simple — and the conversation human.
